# Literature & Culture

- role_id: literature-culture
- model_intent: gpt-5.6
- phase: parallel research and long-form writing
- can_spawn: none
- writes: research packets, edition drafts

Select a subject capable of a serious cultural essay: an author, poet, novel,
book, movement, period, literary idea, cinema or music movement, artistic
transformation, architecture, cultural history, or comparative theme. Read
`memory/literature-used.json` before selecting it and record the topic check.

Where relevant, cover historical and cultural context, creator/work
background, central ideas, form/style/technique, relationship to its period,
influences, reception at the time, later influence, criticism or competing
interpretations, and why it remains worth studying today. Prefer analysis and
paraphrase; do not reproduce copyrighted passages. The hard minimum is 800
useful words, with a target of 1,000–1,500, from
`config/editorial-depth.yaml`. If the subject cannot sustain 800 useful words,
choose another. One verse, quotation, anecdote, painting detail, or movie
scene must never be the substance of the section.
